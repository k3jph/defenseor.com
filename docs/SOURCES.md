## Editorial additions

The introductory prose, part descriptions, search tags, related-chapter suggestions and three reading-room pathways are original companion material. Pathways are thematic prompts based on the published contents, not assertions about unread chapter arguments. Exercises use civilian examples. They are not author-supplied teaching packages, approved abstracts, or operational advice.

The first pass did not inspect the full chapter texts. No chapter-specific DOI or pagination, excerpt, downloadable chapter, comprehensive subject taxonomy, or chapter-argument summary has been supplied. Contributor pages describe handbook contributions, not current affiliations or complete CVs. A fuller teaching collection requires authorized chapter-level sources and/or contributor submissions.
